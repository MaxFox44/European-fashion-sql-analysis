# European-fashion-SQL-analysis

MySQL analysis of sales, customers, products and channels for a European fashion retailer, performing 5 business tasks.

## Key Findings
- Top categories: Dresses and T-Shirts sold over 1,400 units in 2025
- Strongest markets: France and Germany had the highest quarterly revenue in quarter 2
- Strongest demand in April: sizes XS, L and XL lead April sales
- Channel performance: The online store outsold the mobile app in 7 out of 9 sizes 

## Recommendations
- Stock more Dresses and T-Shirts and sizes XS, L and XL
- Focus marketing in France and Germany where revenue is highest
- Focus more on the online store since that's where most sales are from

## Data
- Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/joycemara/european-fashion-store-multitable-dataset

## Tools
MySQL, MySQL Workbench
